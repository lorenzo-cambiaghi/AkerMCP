Music

Every sample below was produced by create_music from the spec beside it. No samples, no recordings, no asset packs: the server renders the WAV and hands it to the engine.

Why this is here. A prototype stops for want of a jump sound as surely as for want of a jump. The synthesiser is sfxr's — pitched noise, a resonant low-pass, duty cycle, arpeggio, punch, retrigger — and the music is a tracker over the same voice, so a melody inherits the filters rather than reimplementing them. Chiptune rather than General MIDI on purpose: the small GM SoundFont everyone reaches for first is GPL, and its samples end up inside the audio produced, which for a tool whose output lands in someone else's game is the wrong thing to inherit.

Copy any spec into create_sound or create_music and it produces exactly the file you just played.
